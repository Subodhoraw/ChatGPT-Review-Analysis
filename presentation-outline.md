# Presentation outline

1. **Introduction:** business question and project objectives.
2. **Dataset:** source, four fields, row count, date coverage, and limitations.
3. **Data preparation:** missing reviews, exact duplicates, repeated IDs, type conversion.
4. **Rating EDA:** rating counts, percentages, and average rating.
5. **Sentiment:** polarity, subjectivity, classification rule, distribution.
6. **Text analysis:** positive/negative terms and phrases; inspect context.
7. **Comparison and time:** rating-versus-sentiment cross-tab and monthly trends.
8. **Recommendations:** insights supported by review examples and measured outputs.
9. **Limitations:** lexicon baseline limitations and dataset coverage.

All numerical findings must be copied from a fresh, successful run of the notebook.
